About these scores and statistics

Stat Score = PPG + 1.35 × RPG + 1.75 × APG + 2.5 × SPG + 2.5 × BPG. Contribution measures production across games played. Score includes the workbook’s award and playoff adjustments. Rankings use the saved values in Jazz Best Seasons.xlsx.

Years identify the season ending year (1997 means 1996–97). Career values use the workbook’s own summaries. Some totals are estimates based on rounded per-game statistics; they may differ from official NBA totals. Playoff box scores have been converted from totals to per-game averages for display. Combined season rows retain the workbook’s combined scores.
